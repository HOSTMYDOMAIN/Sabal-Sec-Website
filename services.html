01

Network Penetration Testing

A manual, adversary-driven test of your external and internal network to identify and exploit weaknesses the way a real attacker would — deeper and more targeted than a vulnerability scan.

1–3 weeks Remote External · Internal

Approach

  • External: reconnaissance, service enumeration, exploitation of internet-facing systems, and credential attacks against exposed services.
  • Internal: host discovery, Active Directory attacks, privilege escalation, lateral movement, and access to critical resources.
  • Manual validation of scanner output to eliminate false positives and prove real impact.
  • Risk-ranking of findings mapped to your business context.

You receive

Executive summary, technical findings with evidence and reproduction steps, a prioritized remediation roadmap, and a live debrief.

02

Application & API Penetration Testing

Web applications and APIs assessed for exploitable flaws across authentication, authorization, input handling and business logic — aligned to the OWASP Testing Guide and ASVS.

1–2 weeks per app set Remote REST · GraphQL · SOAP

Approach

  • Authenticated and unauthenticated testing across every user role.
  • OWASP Top 10 and beyond — access control, injection, SSRF, and the logic flaws scanners miss.
  • API testing for REST, GraphQL and SOAP endpoints.
  • Manual exploitation to demonstrate real data or account exposure.

You receive

Findings by severity with proof-of-concept, remediation guidance per issue, and a debrief with your engineering team.

03

AI Penetration Testing

Assessment of LLM-powered applications, agents and the integrations around them — for the failure modes traditional testing doesn't cover. Aligned to the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and MITRE ATLAS.

1–2 weeks per application Remote LLM · RAG · Agents

Approach

  • Direct and indirect prompt injection, jailbreaks, and system-prompt extraction.
  • Data leakage: sensitive information in responses, retrieval (RAG) and training-data exposure, cross-tenant access.
  • Excessive agency: abuse of tools, function calls and agent workflows to reach real systems and data.
  • Insecure output handling and the application around the model — how model output is trusted downstream, plus the usual auth and API weaknesses.

You receive

Findings with reproducible prompts and evidence, ranked by real impact, with remediation guidance for both the model layer and the application.

04

Social Engineering

Controlled campaigns that test your people and the controls around them — measuring detection and response, not just who clicked.

1–2 weeks per campaign Phishing · Vishing · Pretext

Approach

  • Phishing, and optionally vishing (voice) and pretext scenarios, tailored to your organization.
  • Assessment of email security controls (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), endpoint response, and user reporting.
  • Measurement of detection and response times — not just click rate.
  • Optional payload and endpoint testing under agreed rules of engagement.

You receive

Campaign results, control-effectiveness analysis, and awareness recommendations you can act on.

05

Physical Penetration Testing

On-site testing of physical controls — badges, locks, tailgating and facility access — to evaluate how far an intruder could get in person.

2–5 days per location On-site

Approach

  • Covert entry attempts, tailgating, and badge cloning where authorized.
  • Assessment of reception, visitor and escort procedures.
  • Access to sensitive areas, network jacks and unattended workstations.
  • Conducted under a signed authorization-to-test letter carried on site.

You receive

A narrative of access achieved, evidence, and practical physical-control recommendations.

06

Red Team

A goal-based, multi-vector adversary emulation that combines network, application, social and physical techniques to test your prevention and detection against a realistic threat.

3–6 weeks Objective-driven MITRE ATT&CK

Approach

  • Objective-driven — access to specific data or systems — rather than breadth-first.
  • Stealth and evasion to test detection and response, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Multiple entry vectors chained toward the objective.
  • Optional purple-team collaboration to maximize defender learning.

You receive

Attack narrative, timeline, detection analysis, and strategic recommendations for leadership and your SOC.
